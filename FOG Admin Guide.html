FOG Golf League

Admin & Site Overview Guide — thefog.golf

What Is It?

A mobile-friendly web app for managing the FOG weekly golf league. Handles RSVP, payment tracking, live scoring, skins/payouts, pairings, standings, and a full schedule — all in real time. Players can install it as an app on their home screen.

1. Admin Login

  • Go to thefog.golf
  • Tap the lock icon at the top
  • Password: golf2025
  • This unlocks the Manage Week tab and all admin controls

2. Weekly Setup (Manage Week Tab)

  1. Type the course name — pars auto-fill from the 31+ course master list
  2. Pick Front 9 or Back 9
  3. Set the date and up to 6 tee times
  4. Check CTP holes and pick the Long Drive hole
  5. Click "Save This Week"

3. RSVPs & Pairings

  • Players tap "I'm In" on the This Week or Schedule tab
  • RSVP automatically opens Venmo for dues payment
  • RSVP deadline: Monday 2:00 PM MDT
  • After the deadline, pairings of 4 auto-generate from confirmed players
  • Admin can manually regenerate pairings anytime from Manage Week

4. Scoring Modes

ModeHow It Works
Individual (default)Each player enters their own scores on their phone
GroupOne designated scorer per group enters scores for all 4 players
Toggle between modes in Manage Week. The switch is instant for all connected devices. In group mode, admin picks who scores for each group.

5. Live Scoring

Starting the Round

  1. Click "Open Scoring" in Manage Week to go live
  2. Players pick their name and enter scores hole by hole
  3. Running totals and the full scorecard update in real time for everyone

After the Round

  1. Click "Close & Calculate" — locks scoring, runs skins
  2. Pick CTP winners and Long Drive winner from dropdowns
  3. Low Gross auto-calculates (lowest total score)
  4. The Payout Summary shows everyone's net winnings
Need to fix a score? Click "Reopen Scoring", make edits, then close again.

6. Adding Courses to the Master List

  1. Log in as admin
  2. Go to Manage Week tab
  3. Scroll down to "Manage Courses" (tap to expand)
  4. Enter the course name
  5. Enter all 18 hole pars (Front 9: holes 1–9, Back 9: holes 10–18)
  6. Click "Save Course"
For 9-hole courses (Kennedy, Indian Tree, etc.), enter the same 9 pars for both front and back. The course will appear in autocomplete when setting up a week.

7. Skins Rules

ScenarioResult
One birdie (or better) on a holeBirdie always wins the skin
Multiple birdies on a holeAll birdies split the hole pot evenly
No birdies, one player has lowest scoreLowest unique score wins the skin
No birdies, tied for lowest scoreHole pushes (no winner)
